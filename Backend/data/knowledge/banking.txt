Banking and KYC Scams: Overview
Banking and KYC (Know Your Customer) scams involve fraudulent communications impersonating banks or financial institutions. The goal is to trick users into providing login credentials, OTPs, or debit/credit card numbers.

Common Scam Patterns:
- Scammers send messages claiming the user's bank account or credit card is blocked or suspended due to missing KYC updates.
- Example: "Dear customer, your SBI account has been blocked today. Please update your KYC immediately to reactivate. Click here to verify: https://sbi-verify.xyz/kyc"
- They request the user to click a link leading to a look-alike login page or ask them to download an APK for remote access/verification.

Warning Signs:
- Urgent warnings that your account will be suspended or permanently blocked.
- Request for sensitive information like passwords, UPI PINs, CVV, or One-Time Passwords (OTPs). Note: Banks never ask for these details.
- Domains that look like official banks but use different formats or prefixes/suffixes (e.g., "sbi-kyc.info", "icici-secure.xyz" instead of the official domains "sbi.co.in", "icicibank.com").

Safe Verification Practices:
- Always contact your bank directly through the customer care number listed on the physical bank card or their official website.
- Log in to your banking account only through the official banking app or trusted, verified domains (e.g. sbi.co.in, hdfcbank.com, icicibank.com).
- Report cyber frauds immediately by calling the National Cyber Fraud Helpline at 1930.
