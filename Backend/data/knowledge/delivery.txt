Delivery and Parcel Scams: Overview
Delivery and parcel scams exploit the high volume of online shopping by sending fake shipment updates. These messages claim a delivery failed or is held at customs.

Common Scam Patterns:
- A message claiming a package cannot be delivered because of an incomplete address or unpaid customs fees.
- Example: "Your courier shipment is held at our warehouse due to incorrect address. Please update your address and pay a small verification fee of Rs 15 to reschedule delivery: http://cutt.ly/delivery-tracker"
- The links lead to phishing pages designed to steal credit card details.

Warning Signs:
- Requests for money or payment (even small amounts like Rs 15 or Rs 50) to update addresses or release packages.
- Urgent requests to click on short URLs (e.g. bit.ly, cutt.ly, tinyurl.com) to track or resolve a delivery issue.
- Delivery notifications for packages you never ordered.

Safe Verification Practices:
- Verify tracking numbers directly on the official website of the courier company (e.g., India Post, DHL, FedEx) rather than clicking links in SMS/WhatsApp.
- Never pay fees to release a standard consumer package through unsolicited SMS links.
- Look up the courier's official contact information to verify delivery attempts.
