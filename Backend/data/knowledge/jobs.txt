Job and Recruitment Scams: Overview
Job and recruitment scams prey on job seekers by offering attractive work-from-home jobs or high-salary positions with minimal effort. They ultimately demand upfront fees for training, registration, or equipment.

Common Scam Patterns:
- Offers sent via messaging apps claiming you have been selected for a job with a high daily payout.
- Example: "Congratulations! You are selected for a part-time Work-From-Home job. Earn Rs 3000 to Rs 8000 daily by liking YouTube videos. Contact our HR immediately on WhatsApp."
- Once contacted, scammers request upfront "registration fees", "processing fees", or "training/placement fees" before you can start work or receive your salary.

Warning Signs:
- Job offers that do not require any formal interview or experience.
- Requests for upfront payments of any kind. Reputable employers never charge job seekers fees to apply or work.
- Use of unofficial email addresses (e.g., gmail.com, outlook.com) or communication exclusively via WhatsApp, Telegram, or other messaging channels instead of official corporate domains.

Safe Verification Practices:
- Verify the vacancy directly on the official career portal of the company offering the job.
- Never pay anyone money to secure employment, interviews, or equipment.
- Research the agency or recruiter online to check for reviews, complaints, and official business registration.
